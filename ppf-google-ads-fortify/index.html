Understand the Film

What Is Paint Protection Film?

Paint protection film in Bangalore has become the default choice for owners who won't watch their car's finish fade. PPF is a transparent, high-performance polyurethane film applied to your car's painted surfaces, an invisible, self-healing armour between your paint and everything the road throws at it.

1

How It Works

The film absorbs impact energy, deflects abrasion and resists chemical contamination. That's physical protection, not just a surface coating.

2

Self-Healing Advantage

Heat from sunlight or warm water reforms the film's molecular structure, healing fine scratches without any effort from you.

3

Long-Term Value

Quality film from LLumar, Saint-Gobain or Garware is built to last 7 to 10 years when installed and maintained correctly. Each Fortify job ships with warranty documentation.
