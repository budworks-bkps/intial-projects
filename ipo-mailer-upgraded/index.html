Read about an upcoming IPO in the news?

There’s two questions that usually come to mind:

should I apply, and how do I apply?

Here's how we help you with both.

Tap on IPO button

On the app, go to ‘Stocks’ section.

Scroll down and you’ll find the IPO button.

Pick the one you want

Under the ‘Open’ IPO section, click on the company you’re interested in applying.

Get the full picture

Read through the company’s financials, pros and cons, and the subscription rate. It's a quick way to see how the IPO is doing before you decide.

Choose your lot and apply

Pick how many lots you want and set your bid, or go with the cutoff price. Choose your investor category (regular, employee, or HNI), and apply.

Approve the UPI request

This is the last step to successfully apply for an IPO. The money deducted will be refunded if allotment is not received.
